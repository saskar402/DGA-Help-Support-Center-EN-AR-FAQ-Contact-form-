Frequently Asked Questions

To create an account, click on the 'Sign Up' button in the top right corner and follow the registration process. You'll need a valid email address and a secure password.

Click on 'Forgot Password' on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a password reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to set a new password.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for enterprise customers.

Go to your account settings, navigate to the 'Billing' section, and click 'Update Payment Method'. You can add, remove, or modify your payment information there.

We support the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, please keep your browser updated.
